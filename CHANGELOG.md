# Changelog v1.0.0:
### Initial Release!
### Added Mega Shards & Mega Shard Blocks:
- Mega Shards are a new alternative way of obtaining Mega Stones without having to explore the entire world
- Combine 9 Mega Shards into a Mega Shard Block for easier storage _(and decoration!)_
- Blocks can be crafted back into 9 Mega Shards whenever needed

### Added Mega Energy Deposits:
- Mega Energy Deposits can now be found underground throughout the Overworld
- Deposits contain 1–3 Mega Energy Cores, which slowly grow Mega Energy Buds, similar to budding Amethyst
- Breaking fully grown Clusters rewards Mega Shards, with Fortune increasing the amount you can obtain
- Silk Touch can be used to collect the Buds & Clusters themselves but not the Cores

### Added the Mega Researcher Villager:
- New profession dedicated to researching Mega Energy & selling Mega Stones!
- Uses the new Mega Research Station as its workstation _(check the next section for more info)_
- Sells 2–3 randomly selected Mega Stones per profession level _(Novice to Expert)_, Master-level offering one Tier 5 Mega Stone
- Mega Stones are divided into five tiers based on their competitive viability, with higher tiers costing more Shards _(configurable)_
- Currently includes all 87 Mega Stones in Mega Showdown - across all five tiers, with Tiers 1–4 containing roughly 20 stones each and Tier 5 only having Legendary/Mythical/Blank Mega Stones

### Added the Mega Research Station:
- A new workstation used by the Mega Researcher Villager
- Can also be used to disassemble unwanted Mega Stones back into Mega Shards!
- The amount of Shards you receive depends on the Stone's tier
- You'll receive half of what the Mega Researcher would charge you for the Stone _(configurable)_
- Includes a custom model, GUI, sounds & particles

### Added Research Labs to Villages _(Requires [Lithostitched](https://modrinth.com/mod/lithostitched))_:
- With Lithostitched installed, Mega Researchers can have their own Research Labs in Villages
- These Structures were made by **AIO** so shoutout to him!

### Added lots of Advancements:
- Added a dedicated advancement tab for this mod
- Advancements cover discovering Mega Energy, crafting, trading, collecting Mega Stones & disassembling them
- Includes multiple tier-specific advancements and a few hidden challenges so you don't get bored by the _"grind"_!

### Configurations & Support:
- Added a server-side config for adjusting Mega Stone prices and disassembly returns for all five tiers
- Mega Stone tiers are handled through item tags, allowing datapacks to add or rebalance Stones without modifying the mod itself
- [Lost Lore](https://modrinth.com/datapack/poketwo) v3.2+ already added support for its custom Mega Stones to be traded in the Mega Research Station & sold by the Mega Researcher
